For three years I rebuilt the same matplotlib boilerplate for every quarterly report. Today I'm launching
Tablecraft, the tool I wish my team had.

📊 One CLI: CSV or Parquet in, SVG or PDF chart out
🔁 Every choice lives in a small TOML file next to the data, so charts are reproducible in CI
🔒 MIT-licensed, single binary, no telemetry, no account

A hosted version for teams is coming; the CLI stays free.

If your team still screenshots charts into slide decks, I'd love to hear what would make you switch.
